FAQ

Questions, answered

Don't see yours? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply personally.

How long does a business website take?
It depends on the number of pages and how ready your content is. After our first call you get a clear timeline together with your quote.
Do I need to provide the content?
Share what you have — logo, photos and service details. We help organise and polish the text so it reads well.
Will it work on mobile phones?
Yes. Every site is built mobile-first and tested on phone, tablet and desktop screens.
Can I update it later?
Yes. We can set it up so you edit key content yourself, or we handle updates under a maintenance plan.
